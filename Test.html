How engagements run

Support mapped to where your programme actually is.

Medical affairs needs change by phase — I work project-by-project or as ongoing embedded support, joining wherever the current gap is.

I
Early phase · I–II

Protocol & medical strategy

Protocol review, medical monitoring plans, investigator brochure input, and early scientific narrative for a study that hasn't yet found its voice.

II
Mid phase · II–III

Medical monitoring & safety oversight

Ongoing medical monitoring, safety signal review, data review committee support, and site-facing medical guidance during active recruitment and follow-up.

III
Late phase · III

Medical affairs & KOL engagement

Advisory board design, key opinion leader mapping and engagement, congress strategy, and publication planning ahead of submission.

IV
Post-approval · IV

HCP education & real-world evidence

Medical education materials, HCP-facing content review, real-world evidence generation planning, and regulatory-clinical liaison as the product reaches market.
