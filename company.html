UzcaTech AB

Independent clinical & medical affairs consultancy

UzcaTech AB is a Swedish clinical and medical affairs consultancy built around a simple idea: biotech and pharma teams should be able to bring in senior, independent expertise exactly where and when a programme needs it, without committing to a full-time hire. Engagements can be a single deliverable, an interim role, or ongoing advisory support, scoped to whatever gap actually exists.

The consultancy supports programs across the product lifecycle, from clinical development planning and study design to medical affairs activities, scientific communication, evidence generation, stakeholder engagement, and post-approval support.

Drawing on expertise in pharmacology, pharmacokinetics, real-world evidence, project management, and life science consulting, UzcaTech helps organizations translate complex scientific questions into actionable insights and informed decisions. The consultancy works primarily with clients across Europe and the United States, combining scientific rigor with practical execution.

UzcaTech is built to scale with demand: it currently draws on a small, hand-selected roster of independent experts, starting with its founding consultant, and grows deliberately as new engagements call for additional specialist coverage.
